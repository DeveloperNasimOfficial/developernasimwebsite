Your one-stop commercial furniture manufacturer and upholstery partner.

01

90 Years of Craftsmanship and Trust

QA Group is a nationally trusted commercial furniture manufacturer and upholstery company headquartered in Washington State. With 90 years of experience, we proudly serve upholstery and furniture solutions to restaurants, hospitality groups, and commercial spaces across the United States. What sets us apart is simple – craftsmanship that lasts, precision you can rely on, and service that makes complex projects easier to manage.

02

Simplifying Projects, Bringing Visions to Life

We believe that every project begins with partnership. Whether you’re designing a new space or refreshing an existing one, our role is to simplify the process and bring your vision to life through customizable furniture and upholstery solutions. From made-to-order seating to custom upholstery, we offer solutions that protect your investment and keep every space looking its best.

03

Complete Solutions, Built to Last

With engineered drawings, accurate quotes, and a wide range of finishes and upholstery selections, we make it easy to bring your furniture project together. Our custom furniture capabilities include booths, banquettes, lounge seating, and other furniture built to meet each project’s specifications. And with our warranties and unwavering support, you can count on QA Group to deliver quality, durability, and peace of mind every time.

Why Choose QA Group?

Leave it to the upholstery specialists.

QA Group is uniquely positioned to help you complete your next project on time, on budget, and on design – done correctly the first time.

Our team and facilities can handle any job scope, from one piece to dozens. We operate specialized departments staffed by experts in frame building, woodworking, sewing, upholstery, wood staining and finishing, as well as product assembly and installation.

We are problem solvers; clients trust and rely on us to turn their ideas into beautiful yet functional realities. Additionally, our reputation as the pinnacle of quality has made us a top choice for leading design firms and architects. Serving clients nationwide, we offer unparalleled custom upholstery of the highest caliber.

Clients of QA Group can rely on unmatched attention to detail throughout our extensive range of custom upholstery services. And because our commercial-grade furniture is manufactured right here in the USA, we make sure the job is perfect in every detail from beginning to end.

01

Reliable Turnaround Times

Standard lead time is 6 to 10 weeks, depending on project size. This timeline begins once we have final drawing approvals or all required materials – whichever is later.

Production begins only after we’ve received the deposit (if required), confirmed all materials, and obtained customer approval on dimensions and engineering.

02

Premium Quality Craftsmanship

Attention to detail is ingrained in our ethos. Furthermore, we leave no stone unturned in our commitment to quality upholstery services. Every project undergoes meticulous inspection to prevent any inaccuracies before construction.

03

Exceptional Customer Service

Your satisfaction is our priority. With a dedicated quality control team and a commitment to transparent, honest practices, we stand behind our work. Make us your trusted partner; our adaptable, flexible solutions are here to support your goals.
