# QA Group homepage: concept round 3 (compact)

Based on Skylar's clarification: keep everything that's on the homepage today, and reorganize it into a shorter, cleaner, more intentional layout.

## Ground rules

- **Copy:** every word, heading, button and link from the current staging homepage, unchanged. Nothing added and nothing removed (checked by script).
- **Images:** only the images on the homepage today. The extra photos and the footer address from round 2 have been taken out.
- **Length:** about **5,250px** at desktop width, compared with about 6,500px in round 1 and 8,000px in round 2.

**Style:**
- **Brand guide:** Deep Brown `#534132`, Golden Mustard `#D4B858` accents, Brandon Grotesque headings, Garamond body. EB Garamond stands in until the Adobe Garamond Pro kit is added.
- **Details:** hairline dividers and small uppercase labels.
- **Motion:** soft fade-up reveals on scroll.
- **Header:** transparent over the hero, then solid white after scrolling, with simple dropdowns.
